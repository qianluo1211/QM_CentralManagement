[h1]Central Material Management 1.3.0 — Interface Rework and Loadout Preset Fix[/h1]

[h2]Important fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Fixed loadout presets not saving. Presets were lost on every exit: Unity's JsonUtility silently dropped the entire preset list and wrote only the version and the selected id. Storage is now a hand-written line format at [b]AppData\LocalLow\Magnum Scriptum Ltd\Quasimorph\QM_CentralManagement_LoadoutPresets.txt[/b], which you can read, back up, and share. Every save writes a temporary file, reads it back, and only replaces the real one once the presets are confirmed intact, so an empty payload can never overwrite your data.
[*]Fixed mechanical limbs being filed under Weapons. The 24 body-part replacements (cyborg hands and legs, the drill arm, spider claw, the possessed and Quasi limbs) carry both a weapon record and an augmentation record, and were classified as weapons -- so fitting an agent meant switching to the Weapons tab to find them. They are now classified by their augmentation record and removed from Weapons.
[/list]

[h2]Interface rework[/h2]
[list]
[*]The panel now uses the game's own UI art throughout: nine-slice frames, real hover/pressed/disabled button sprites, the vanilla click sound, and the game's palette and font material, replacing flat colour blocks and a blurred outline effect.
[*]Item cards rebuilt: the name gets a full line of its own and the wide layout drops from six columns to four, roughly doubling the room for names and allowing the game's standard body text size instead of a shrunken font.
[*]Category tabs now split across rows based on panel width and stretch edge to edge, removing the ragged empty cells.
[*]Added a recessed well behind the card grid so the panel reads as layers rather than one flat plane.
[*]Panel height now follows the actual canvas height, fixing the panel running off screen on ultrawide displays.
[*]Vanilla item icons are no longer scaled, fixing blurred and stretched icon art.
[/list]

[h2]Other fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Fixed button captions not centring and overflowing into neighbouring controls.
[*]Fixed two-cell item icons spilling outside their card.
[*]Fixed the CLOSE button extending past the panel frame.
[*]Fixed the footer status readout overlapping the buttons in the narrow layout.
[*]Fixed the sort and slot buttons resizing with their selected text, which made the search field jump under the cursor.
[*]Status counts now use thousands separators.
[/list]

[h2]Usability[/h2]
[list]
[*]Augments gained two sub-filters, Limbs and Implants: limbs replace a body part, implants slot into one.
[*]Switching augment sub-filters now keeps the selected body slot instead of resetting it to All.
[*]The preset selector, agent selector, sort and slot controls share one vanilla dropdown appearance with a caret. The preset and agent names used to be plain text with no frame, giving no sign they could be clicked.
[*]The preset list now opens directly beneath its selector instead of covering the preset summary.
[/list]
