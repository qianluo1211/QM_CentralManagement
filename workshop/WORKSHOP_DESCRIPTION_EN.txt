[h1]CENTRAL MATERIAL MANAGEMENT[/h1]

[b]Stop opening storage bays 1 through 7 one by one. Turn the entire ship inventory into one searchable, sortable logistics terminal.[/b]

[quote]
Loadout presets are available from the start: the bar above the agent equipment screen saves, switches, and applies full loadouts (augments and mechanical limbs included) with no technology required. Research the major Central Logistics Matrix technology in Supply to unlock the central terminal. Since 1.5.0 that technology also replaces the station trade screen with a bulk market.
[/quote]

[h2]Quick Start[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Save, switch, and apply loadouts from the bar above the agent equipment screen — no technology required.
[*]Research [b]Central Logistics Matrix[/b] below Recycling in the Supply tree.
[*]Use the spaceship button or press [b]C[/b]. The mission briefing has the same entry and shortcut.
[*]Search, filter, and sort. Mouse wheel changes pages.
[*]Open Agent Gear for the most recently deployed available agent; click the name to switch.
[*]Drag items between central storage and the agent. Right-click a stack and use vanilla Split to pick up an exact quantity.
[*]With Fast trade enabled, visit a station for the bulk trade panel.
[*]Built the cargo shuttle? Load it once, save a manifest, and it refills before every deployment.
[/olist]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Station trade panel[/b]: Replaces vanilla station trade. Buy lists the station's full stock; sell lists only what it accepts and aggregates your storages. One cart, one Trade button (sells first, then buys). Prices follow vanilla formulas. Wheel pages; hold left and sweep for bulk −/+/MAX (×10/×100/×1000). Shortcuts: B, [ ], D, Delete, A. Quest deliveries show what the station handed back, with item icons.
[*][b]Shuttle restock manifests[/b]: The game empties the cargo shuttle after every raid. Save the hold as a named list and refill in one click, or let it top up automatically as the raid launches. It only adds what is missing. Manage it from the pre-departure shuttle tab or from the central panel.
[*][b]Ship-wide index[/b]: Storage bays 1–7, built cold storage, and available recycling storage.
[*][b]Search and filters[/b]: Names or internal IDs, then Weapons, Equipment, Ammo, Ordnance, Supplies, Augments, Materials, Barter, Special.
[*][b]Responsive catalog[/b]: Nearly full-screen with gear hidden; fits the remaining right side when the agent pane is open. Header dropdown switches agent gear, shuttle hold, augmentation, or hidden.
[*][b]Direct agent management[/b]: Last deployed available agent, name dropdown, two-way drag-and-drop.
[*][b]Loadout presets[/b]: From the very start, no technology required. Weapons, armor, backpack and vest with exact counts, mechanical parts, socket capacity, and implants. Same tools in the central panel and on the after-raid screen (applying a preset there is a selective unload).
[*][b]Mission briefing[/b]: Opens on the operator already selected, gear expanded. Close and you are back on the briefing.
[*][b]One-click repair[/b]: Mends the selected agent's damaged gear from ship storage (kits they are carrying are left alone). Kits before scrap parts; nothing is repaired to destruction. Confirms first, then shows what was spent.
[*][b]Safe partial application[/b]: If only stock is missing you may continue: empty slots stay empty, missing carried items are skipped, incomplete body configs leave the current body.
[*][b]Exact stacks[/b]: Vanilla right-click Split puts that quantity on the cursor.
[*][b]Contextual sorting[/b]: Name, quantity, or tech level (every category); equipment by resist; weapons by damage; ammo by multiplier; augments by installation slot. Armor sets read the game's ArmorSets data.
[*][b]Augmentation[/b]: Open the selected agent's vanilla Augmentation screen from the catalog. Install rules stay vanilla.
[*][b]Batch recycling[/b]: Left-click FULL for a whole type, right-click for an exact count. Disabled while the recycler is busy; skips locked, quest, hidden-weapon, non-recyclable, and already queued items.
[*][b]Mouse[/b]: Wheel paging, wheel on the split slider, click-through protection for dialogs and dropdowns.
[/list]

[code]debugLogging=false
shortcutKey=C
raidPrepCentral=true
shipLoadouts=true

stationTrade=true
autoUnlockTech=false
tradeConfirm=false

[code]shortcutKey=None[/code] disables the spaceship shortcut. Restart the game after editing the file. With MCM subscribed, the main flags can also be changed in-game.
